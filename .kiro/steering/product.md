# Product Overview

This is a luxury wine e-commerce platform that accepts cryptocurrency payments, targeting crypto investors, wine collectors, and enthusiasts globally.

## Core Features
- Premium wine catalog with detailed product information
- Cryptocurrency payment processing (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE, LITE, USDC, USDT)
- Traditional payment options (Euros)
- Global VIP delivery with insurance options
- Wine NFTs and digital certificates
- Private sales and exclusive collections
- Affiliate and loyalty programs
- Mobile-responsive design

## Target Users
- Wine enthusiasts and collectors
- Crypto investors looking to spend digital assets
- Global customers requiring premium delivery services
- VIP customers seeking exclusive wine access

## Business Model
- Direct wine sales with crypto and fiat payments
- Premium delivery and insurance services
- Exclusive Wine NFT marketplace
- Private sales for rare wines
- Affiliate commission program

## Key Differentiators
- Cryptocurrency payment integration
- Luxury aesthetic and user experience
- Global shipping with VIP services
- Blockchain-based wine certificates (NFTs)
- Exclusive access to rare wines
